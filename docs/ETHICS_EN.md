# Ethics (ETHICS_EN)

IfWe deals with real relationships and real emotions, which sets a few
non-negotiable boundaries for this project.

## 1. Built for reflection, not persuasion

The value of this tool is helping **the user** understand a relationship,
practice expressing things, and make peace with regret. It is not suitable —
and must not be used — for:

- rehearsing manipulative lines aimed at the real person;
- replacing direct, honest communication with them;
- judging or prosecuting their behavior.

## 2. Against "digital necromancy"

Keeping a lost relationship alive forever inside a model is self-harm with
extra steps. We suggest:

- setting usage boundaries for yourself (e.g. fixed weekly time);
- treating replays as another form of writing — write it, then close it;
- if you notice you are avoiding real life in favor of replays, stop and
  consider professional support (resources in [../DISCLAIMER_EN.md](../DISCLAIMER_EN.md)).

## 3. The data involves real people

Imported chat logs contain another person's words and life. Please:

- process such data only lawfully and in good faith;
- never publish it, spread it, or use it for training or harassment
  (explicitly forbidden by the additional terms in LICENSE);
- blur every identifying detail before sharing screenshots or results.

## 4. Risks of AI-generated content

Everything the persona says is improvisation over the memories you provided.
It will confidently make things up, and it may say things the real person
would never say. Stay clear-eyed: this is fiction, not a transcript.

## 5. Self-restraints built into the design

- Replays are physically isolated from real history (`sim_*` namespaces); the
  UI labels estimates as "模拟估计量 · 非事实" (simulation estimate, not fact);
- Relationship state is presented with low confidence and no verdicts; the UI
  never asserts what the other person "really thinks";
- The privacy gate (check_privacy) is a hard release criterion — we would
  rather delay a release than ship with a privacy hit;
- No scraping capability is bundled, and no shortcuts for obtaining other
  people's data are provided.

## 6. If you are going through it

This project grew out of a real loss. If you are reading it from a similar
place right now: a tool can only accompany you as far as tools go. The rest of
the road belongs to real people — friends, family, or professionals. Helplines:
[../DISCLAIMER_EN.md](../DISCLAIMER_EN.md).
