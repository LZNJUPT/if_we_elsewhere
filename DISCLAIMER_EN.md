## 2. This is not prediction

Every "IF branch" is **fictional improvisation** by a language model. It does
not predict what a real person will do or where a real relationship will go.
Real people have unpredictable free will.

## 3. Generated content never represents the real person

The digital persona acts on a personality profile derived from your records.
However convincing it looks, it is **not** an expression of the other person's
actual will. Do not use replayed conversations as a basis for communicating
with them, making life decisions, or judging them.

## 4. Your responsibility for others' privacy

Before importing chat logs involving other people, make sure what you are
doing complies with applicable law and respects everyone involved. Using this
software to track, harass, or monitor real people is **strictly forbidden**
(see the additional terms in LICENSE).

## 5. LLM and third parties

When you enable conversations, sanitized text is sent to the LLM API provider
**you configure yourself**, and is subject to their privacy policy and terms.
This software ships no API keys and proxies no LLM service.
